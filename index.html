Educational Atlas of 3D Bones

Downloadable 3D bone models for teaching and learning.

This page and the models are still under development and not yet usable.

About

This atlas provides publicly available 3D models of human bones for educational purposes — anatomy teaching, visualization, 3D printing, and student projects. Each bone is offered both as a surface mesh and as a CAD model.
