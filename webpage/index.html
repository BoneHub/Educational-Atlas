Educational Atlas

Two complete human skeletons, free STL meshes and IGES/STEP CAD models for education.

About

This atlas provides publicly available 3D models of human bones for educational purposes — anatomy teaching, visualization, 3D printing, and student projects. The models cover the full skeletons of the Visible Human Male and Visible Human Female, segmented bone by bone from CT. Each bone is offered as a surface mesh (STL) and as a NURBS CAD model (IGES and STEP).

The files are hosted in the visible-human-3d-models dataset on Hugging Face (CC BY 4.0). If you use them, please see Citation for what to cite.
